About LifeOS

LifeOS is a cloud-hosted personal productivity software application operated by Luke Sheaffer, a sole proprietor based in the United States. The application connects to a user's task list and calendar and automatically generates task reminders, schedule alerts, and priority notifications. These notifications are delivered by SMS text message, through the Twilio messaging platform, to users who have explicitly enrolled. LifeOS sends only account-related notifications: it does not send marketing or promotional messages, and it never messages anyone who has not opted in.

How to opt in - method 1: text us first

1. From your own mobile phone, send the keyword START to +1 (201) 762-0827, the LifeOS notification number.

2. By texting START you agree to receive recurring automated task and schedule notification text messages from LifeOS. Message frequency varies; typically a few messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

3. You will receive a single enrollment confirmation message with HELP and STOP instructions. No further messages are sent until your LifeOS account is configured.

How to opt in - method 2: LifeOS application settings

1. In the LifeOS application's notification settings, enter your mobile phone number.

2. Check the consent checkbox, which is unchecked by default and reads exactly: "I agree to receive recurring automated text messages from LifeOS at the mobile number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help."

3. Verify ownership of the number by confirming the verification message LifeOS sends to it. Notifications begin only after verification.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any LifeOS message at any time to unsubscribe; you will receive one final confirmation message and nothing further. Reply HELP for assistance, or email the contact below.
