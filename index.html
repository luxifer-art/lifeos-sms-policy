LifeOS SMS Notifications

LifeOS is a personal productivity application that sends automated task reminders, schedule alerts, and priority notifications by SMS to enrolled users.

How to enroll

Text START to +1 (201) 762-0827 from your mobile phone to opt in to LifeOS notifications, or enter and verify your mobile number in the LifeOS application settings.

By enrolling you agree to receive automated SMS notifications from LifeOS. Message frequency varies; typically a few messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

How to stop

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance.
