Program description

The LifeOS SMS program delivers personal task reminders, schedule alerts, and priority notifications from LifeOS, a personal productivity system operated by Luke Sheaffer as a sole proprietor. Messages are sent only to phone numbers explicitly enrolled and verified by their owner.

Opting in

You can opt in by texting START to the LifeOS number from your phone, or by entering and verifying your mobile number in the LifeOS system configuration. By enrolling, you consent to receive automated SMS notifications at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies; recipients typically receive a few messages per day. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to cancel and stop receiving messages. After opting out you will receive a single confirmation message. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact luke.sheaffer@gmail.com.

Privacy

Mobile information is never shared with or sold to third parties. See the LifeOS SMS Privacy Policy for details.

Eligibility and acceptable use

The program is intended for personal use by the LifeOS system owner and enrolled users aged 18 or older in the United States.
